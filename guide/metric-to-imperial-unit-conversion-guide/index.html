The Complete Unit Conversion Reference

Whether you are cooking a recipe from a foreign cookbook, traveling abroad, or working on a science project, knowing how to convert between metric and imperial units is an essential skill. This guide covers the most common conversions with exact factors and practical tips for remembering them.

Length Conversions

Metric to Imperial

  • 1 millimeter (mm) = 0.03937 inches
  • 1 centimeter (cm) = 0.3937 inches
  • 1 meter (m) = 3.28084 feet = 1.09361 yards
  • 1 kilometer (km) = 0.62137 miles = 3,280.84 feet

Imperial to Metric

  • 1 inch = 2.54 cm (exact, by definition)
  • 1 foot = 30.48 cm = 0.3048 m
  • 1 yard = 91.44 cm = 0.9144 m
  • 1 mile = 1,609.34 m = 1.60934 km

Quick memory tricks:

  • 1 inch is about 2.5 cm (close enough for estimates)
  • 1 meter is about 3.3 feet (or just over 1 yard)
  • 1 km is about 0.6 miles (or 5/8 of a mile)

Weight Conversions

Metric to Imperial

  • 1 gram (g) = 0.03527 ounces
  • 100 grams = 3.527 ounces
  • 1 kilogram (kg) = 2.20462 pounds = 35.274 ounces
  • 1 metric ton = 2,204.62 pounds

Imperial to Metric

  • 1 ounce (oz) = 28.3495 grams
  • 1 pound (lb) = 453.592 grams = 0.453592 kg
  • 1 stone (UK) = 14 pounds = 6.35029 kg
  • 1 US ton = 2,000 pounds = 907.185 kg

Quick memory tricks:

  • 1 kg is about 2.2 pounds
  • 1 pound is about 450 grams
  • 100 grams is about 3.5 ounces

Volume Conversions

Metric to Imperial (US)

  • 1 milliliter (ml) = 0.033814 fluid ounces
  • 100 ml = 3.3814 fluid ounces
  • 1 liter (L) = 33.814 fluid ounces = 4.22675 cups = 2.11338 pints = 1.05669 quarts = 0.264172 gallons

Imperial (US) to Metric

  • 1 fluid ounce = 29.5735 ml
  • 1 cup = 236.588 ml
  • 1 pint = 473.176 ml
  • 1 quart = 946.353 ml
  • 1 US gallon = 3,785.41 ml = 3.78541 L

Note: UK (imperial) measurements differ from US measurements:

  • 1 UK pint = 568 ml (vs 473 ml for US pint)
  • 1 UK gallon = 4.546 L (vs 3.785 L for US gallon)

Temperature Conversion

The two formulas you need:

  • Celsius to Fahrenheit: F = (C x 9/5) + 32
  • Fahrenheit to Celsius: C = (F - 32) x 5/9

Key Reference Points

  • -40: Same in both scales (-40C = -40F)
  • 0C = 32F (water freezes)
  • 20C = 68F (comfortable room temperature)
  • 37C = 98.6F (normal body temperature)
  • 100C = 212F (water boils at sea level)

Quick estimate: Double the Celsius temperature and add 30 to get approximate Fahrenheit. (20C x 2 + 30 = 70F; actual is 68F — close enough for weather.)

Area Conversions

  • 1 square meter (m2) = 10.7639 square feet
  • 1 square foot = 0.0929 m2
  • 1 hectare = 10,000 m2 = 2.47105 acres
  • 1 acre = 4,046.86 m2 = 0.404686 hectares
  • 1 square kilometer = 0.386102 square miles
  • 1 square mile = 2.58999 km2

Speed Conversions

  • 1 km/h = 0.621371 mph
  • 1 mph = 1.60934 km/h
  • 1 meter per second = 3.6 km/h = 2.23694 mph

Common speed reference points:

  • 50 km/h = 31 mph (typical city speed limit)
  • 100 km/h = 62 mph (typical highway speed limit in metric countries)
  • 120 km/h = 75 mph

Cooking Measurement Conversions

Volume

  • 1 teaspoon = 5 ml
  • 1 tablespoon = 15 ml = 3 teaspoons
  • 1 fluid ounce = 30 ml = 2 tablespoons
  • 1 cup = 240 ml = 16 tablespoons
  • 1 pint = 480 ml = 2 cups

Weight

  • 1 stick of butter = 113 grams = 1/2 cup = 8 tablespoons
  • 1 cup of flour = approximately 120-130 grams (varies by packing)
  • 1 cup of sugar = approximately 200 grams

Oven Temperatures

  • 325F = 165C (slow oven)
  • 350F = 175C (moderate oven, most common baking temperature)
  • 375F = 190C
  • 400F = 200C (hot oven)
  • 425F = 220C (very hot oven)

Tips for Memorizing Key Conversions

Focus on a handful of anchor conversions and derive others from them:

  • 1 inch = 2.54 cm (exact — memorize this one)
  • 1 kg = 2.2 lbs (close enough for everyday use)
  • 1 mile = 1.6 km (or 5/8 of a mile per km)
  • 1 liter = about 1 quart (actually 1.057 quarts)
  • 0C = 32F, 100C = 212F (derive everything else from these two points)
