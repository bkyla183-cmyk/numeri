About Numeriq & Our Mathematical Editorial Standards

Learn about Numeriq editorial principles, 2026 regulatory benchmarks, double-precision formulas, and multi-stage verification methodology.

Numeriq was created to connect published mathematical, financial, clinical, and scientific equations with everyday decision-making—offering 500 client-side calculators with transparent equations, worked examples, and 45 explanatory guides.

1. Published Regulatory & Scientific Equations

Financial, tax, retirement, clinical, and engineering calculators implement primary published standards including IRS Revenue Procedure 2025-32 and IRS Notice 2025-67 (2026 brackets, $16,100/$32,200 standard deductions, $24,500 401(k) and $7,500 IRA limits), HMRC 2026–27 allowances, CRA 2026 RRSP/TFSA rules, ATO FY 2026–27 Superannuation thresholds, CDC/WHO health standards, and NIST/CODATA physical constants.

2. Independent Multi-Stage Verification

  • Primary Source Grounding: Every regulatory rule and physical constant is documented with its effective date and authoritative institutional link.
  • Independent Arithmetic Checks: Worked examples explicitly declare compounding frequency, payment timing, and intermediate rounding steps.
  • Boundary & Edge-Case Guards: All calculators validate zero divisors, negative domains, empty inputs, and extreme magnitudes cleanly without NaN or Infinity leaks.
  • Client-Side Privacy: Computations execute strictly in your browser using IEEE 754 double-precision JavaScript arithmetic without transmitting entered numbers to external servers.
