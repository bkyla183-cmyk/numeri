Privacy Policy (GDPR, UK GDPR & CCPA Compliant)

Numeriq privacy policy: client-side calculation privacy, local browser storage, Google AdSense advertising cookies, GDPR, CCPA, and PIPEDA rights.

Last Updated: November 20, 2026

1. Client-Side Calculation Privacy

Numeriq executes all calculator computations directly inside your browser. Financial figures, tax salaries, health measurements, and dates entered into tools are never stored on or transmitted to an application database.

2. First-Party Browser Storage

Numeriq uses non-personal browser localStorage keys strictly to remember optional UI preferences such as light/dark theme (calc_theme), bookmarked tools/articles (calc_saved_calcs, calc_saved_posts), and local usage sorting (numeriq_calc_usage), which you can clear anytime.

3. Advertising, Cookies & Third-Party Disclosures

When display advertising (such as Google AdSense) is active following domain approval and regional consent verification, third-party vendors including Google may use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits. Visitors may review Google's advertising technology practices and opt out of personalized ads via Google Ads Policies.

4. International Privacy Rights (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA)

Numeriq does not sell personal data. Visitors in the EEA, UK, California and other US states, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand retain full consent, access, and opt-out controls. Contact us anytime through our Contact page.
