How It Works & Mathematical Principles

A triangle area calculator computes planar triangular area using Heron’s three-side formula (default), Base × Perpendicular Height, or Side-Angle-Side (SAS) trigonometry.

Whether surveying an irregular land parcel with three tape-measured sides (Heron’s formula), framing a roof gable from base and altitude (0.5 × b × h), or measuring two beams meeting at a known angle (0.5 × a × b × sin γ), selecting the matching geometry mode yields exact area without manual altitude conversions.

Governing Equation
Heron: √[s(s - a)(s - b)(s - c)] | Base×Height: 0.5 × b × h | SAS: 0.5 × a × b × sin(γ)

Step-by-Step Worked Calculation Example

Scenario: Calculating the area, perimeter, and classification of a scalene triangle with sides 5 cm, 6 cm, and 7 cm via Heron’s Formula.

  • Compute semi-perimeter s = (5 + 6 + 7) / 2 = 9 cm (Perimeter = 18 cm).
  • Apply Heron’s Formula: Area = √[9 × (9 - 5) × (9 - 6) × (9 - 7)] = √[9 × 4 × 3 × 2] = √216.
  • Evaluate √216 = 14.6969 sq units (Scalene triangle). By comparison, Base 10 × Height 6 mode gives 0.5 × 10 × 6 = 30 sq units.

Result: 14.6969 sq units (Perimeter = 18 cm; Scalene)

Heron’s formula avoids needing to measure vertical altitude, while Base × Height and SAS modes handle architectural and trigonometric layouts directly.

Substituting Slant Side Length for Perpendicular Height

Using a slanted side instead of true vertical height overestimates triangular area on non-right triangles.

Frequently Asked Questions

When should you use Heron’s formula instead of ½ × base × height?

Heron’s formula Area = √[s(s − a)(s − b)(s − c)] lets you find the exact area of any triangle knowing only its three side lengths a, b, and c—without measuring its perpendicular altitude.

What is the triangle inequality condition?

For three side lengths to form a valid closed triangle, the sum of any two sides must strictly exceed the third side (a + b > c, a + c > b, b + c > a).

What does semi-perimeter s represent in Heron’s formula?

Semi-perimeter s equals half the triangle’s perimeter: s = (a + b + c) / 2.
