How It Works & Mathematical Principles

The One-Rep Max (1RM) & Training Load Calculator averages the three most widely validated resistance-training equations—Brzycki, Epley, and Lombardi—to predict true maximal single-repetition strength and prescribe exact working weights at 90%, 80%, and 70% training intensities.

Attempting an unspotted, maximal 1-rep barbell squat, bench press, or deadlift carries unnecessary injury risk for most lifters. Estimating 1RM from a clean, high-effort submaximal set of 2–10 repetitions provides near-identical programming accuracy without risking form breakdown. Averaging Brzycki (conservative above 6 reps), Epley (linear), and Lombardi (exponential) produces a balanced composite 1RM plus relative strength-to-bodyweight ratio.

Governing Equation
Brzycki = W × 36 ÷ (37 − R) | Epley = W × (1 + R ÷ 30) | Lombardi = W × R^0.10

Step-by-Step Worked Calculation Example

Scenario: A 180 lb lifter completes 5 clean repetitions of 225 lb on the barbell bench press.

  • Brzycki: 225 × (36 ÷ (37 − 5)) = 225 × 1.125 = 253.1 lb.
  • Epley: 225 × (1 + 5 ÷ 30) = 225 × 1.1667 = 262.5 lb.
  • Lombardi: 225 × 5^0.10 = 225 × 1.1746 = 264.3 lb.
  • Composite Mean: (253.1 + 262.5 + 264.3) ÷ 3 = 260.0 lb (1.44× Bodyweight); 90% = 234.0 lb, 80% = 208.0 lb, 70% = 182.0 lb.

Result: 260.0 lb Composite 1RM (1.44× BW · 80% Working Set = 208.0 lb)

Reaching 225 × 5 corresponds closely to a ~260 lb tested max; hypertrophy volume blocks at 70%–80% prescribe 182–208 lb working sets.

Using High-Rep Sets (15–25 Reps) or Bounced Cheated Reps to Project Heavy Squat/Deadlift 1RMs

Cardiovascular fatigue, lactate buffering, and momentum inflate high-rep 1RM projections; restrict estimation sets to ≤ 8 strict technical reps.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does this calculator average Brzycki, Epley, and Lombardi instead of showing only one formula?

Each formula behaves slightly differently across rep ranges: Epley tracks linearly, Brzycki steepens slightly past 8 reps, and Lombardi scales smoothly at low reps. Averaging all three cancels individual curve bias across 2–10 repetitions.

Why is the repetition input capped at 15 clean reps?

Repetition-to-1RM prediction equations lose physiological validity beyond 12–15 reps because high-rep failure measures muscular glycogen endurance rather than peak maximal neural recruitment.

How do I use the 70%, 80%, and 90% training loads shown in the results?

Use 85%–90%+ for low-rep maximal strength blocks (2–4 reps), 75%–82% for balanced power/hypertrophy sets (6–8 reps), and 65%–72% for higher-volume hypertrophy work (10–12 reps).
