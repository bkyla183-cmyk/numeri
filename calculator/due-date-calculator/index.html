How It Works & Mathematical Principles

A Pregnancy Due Date Calculator estimates Estimated Delivery Date (EDD), current gestational age in weeks and days, trimester progression, and key prenatal screening milestones from the first day of the Last Menstrual Period (LMP) adjusted for cycle length.

Standard obstetric dating adds 280 days (40 weeks) to the first day of the last menstrual period for a classic 28-day cycle, shifting slightly earlier or later when ovulation occurs before or after day 14. First-trimester ultrasound crown-rump length remains the clinical gold standard for confirming dating.

Governing Equation
EDD = LMP + 280 Days + (Cycle Length - 28) Days (Naegele’s Rule)

Step-by-Step Worked Calculation Example

Scenario: Evaluating remaining days at 65 days elapsed since LMP on a standard 28-day cycle.

  • Total standard gestational span = 280 days (40 weeks).
  • Gestational age = 9 weeks and 2 days (First Trimester).
  • Days remaining until EDD = 280 - 65 = 215 days.

Result: 215 Days Until Estimated Due Date (9w 2d Gestational Age)

Only ~4% of spontaneous births occur on the exact EDD; healthy full-term delivery spans weeks 39 through 40+6.

Using Cycle End Date or Conception Date Instead of LMP Day 1

Gestational weeks count from the very first day of your last menstrual bleeding—roughly two weeks prior to actual fertilization.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Naegele’s Rule for estimating a pregnancy due date?

Naegele’s Rule adds 280 days (40 weeks) to the first day of the Last Menstrual Period (LMP), adjusted by adding or subtracting days if your typical menstrual cycle differs from 28 days.

How accurate is an LMP-based Estimated Delivery Date?

Only about 4%–5% of births occur on the exact EDD, though most spontaneous full-term deliveries happen between 37 and 42 weeks; first-trimester ultrasound measurements supersede LMP if dates differ significantly.

When do the three pregnancy trimesters begin and end?

Clinically under ACOG conventions, Trimester 1 spans Weeks 0–13, Trimester 2 spans Weeks 14–27, and Trimester 3 runs from Week 28 until delivery.
