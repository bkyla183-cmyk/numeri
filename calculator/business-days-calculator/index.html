How It Works & Mathematical Principles

A Business Days Calculator counts net working days between two dates excluding weekends and custom holidays, and can also project a target delivery date by adding or subtracting business days under Mon–Fri or Sun–Thu workweeks.

Shipping deadlines, bank clearing times (ACH / wire T+2), and legal response timelines count business days rather than raw calendar days. Entering comma-separated YYYY-MM-DD holiday dates automatically deducts weekday bank closures without manual adjustments.

Governing Equation
Business Days = Count of dates d ∈ [Start, End] where d is a workday and d ∉ Holidays

Step-by-Step Worked Calculation Example

Scenario: Counting Monday–Friday business days across October 2026 (2026-10-01 through 2026-10-31).

  • Total inclusive calendar days in October = 31 days.
  • October 2026 contains 4 Sundays plus 5 Saturdays (Oct 3, 10, 17, 24, 31) = 9 weekend days.
  • Net Monday–Friday working days = 31 - 9 = 22 Business Days.

Result: 22 Business Days (9 weekend days excluded)

Add any regional public bank holidays (e.g., 2026-10-12) in Advanced Options to automatically subtract them from 22 when scheduling payroll or banking.

Forgetting Regional Public Holidays During Month-Long Sprints

Months like November or December can carry 1–3 weekday bank holidays that reduce effective capacity below raw weekday counts.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between calendar days and business days?

Calendar days count every day including Saturdays, Sundays, and public holidays, whereas business days count only Monday–Friday working days excluding listed public holidays.

Why are holidays subtracted after counting weekdays?

If an observed public holiday falls on a weekday, it reduces net shipping or SLA working days by 1. Holidays falling on Saturday or Sunday are already excluded by weekend filtering.

Where are business-day calculations used most often?

They govern ACH bank clearing windows, shipping transit SLAs, contract notice deadlines, and corporate PTO accrual planning.
