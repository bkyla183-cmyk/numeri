How It Works & Mathematical Principles

The Lease vs Buy Car Calculator delivers an apples-to-apples multi-year cost comparison over the exact same driving horizon—translating Lease Money Factors into implied APR (MF × 2,400) while crediting projected vehicle resale equity to the purchase path.

Comparing a $587/mo lease payment directly against a $735/mo auto loan payment is misleading because after 36 months the lessee returns the car with zero equity (plus disposition fee), whereas the buyer owns an asset worth far more than the remaining loan payoff. This model equates both paths over the exact same term using identical residual/resale values and sales tax rules.

Governing Equation
LeaseMo = [(AdjCap − Res)/N + (AdjCap + Res)×MF]×(1+Tax%) | BuyNetCost = Down + Σ PMT − (Resale − LoanBal_N)

Step-by-Step Worked Calculation Example

Scenario: Evaluating a $40,000 car over 36 months (58% value retained = $23,200; 7% tax): Lease ($2,500 down, 0.0025 MF = 6.0% APR, $1,095 fees) vs. Buy ($5,000 down, 6.25% APR for 60 months).

  • Lease: Implied APR = 0.0025 × 2,400 = 6.00%; Pre-tax payment = $548.97 → With 7% tax = $587.40/mo; Total 36-mo Lease Net Cost (with down + tax + fees) = $24,916.41.
  • Buy: Financing $37,800 ($40k + $2,800 tax − $5,000 down) @ 6.25% (60 mos) = $735.18/mo. After 36 months, cash out = $31,466.55.
  • At Month 36, remaining loan balance = $16,545.73 vs. $23,200 resale value → Equity = $6,654.27 → Buy Net Cost = $24,812.28.

Result: $24,812.28 Net 36-Mo Buy Cost vs. $24,916.41 Lease (+$104.13 Buying Edge at Yr 3)

Even over just 3 years, buying edges out leasing by $104—and keeping the car through Year 5–8 widens buying’s advantage dramatically once payments end.

Methodology, Verification & Limitations

Verified: 2026-11-19 · 2026 US Consumer Leasing Act (Reg M)

Numeriq Consumer Finance Editorial Team · Reviewed by Numeriq Quantitative Validation Desk

Equates lease net cash outflow against purchase outlays less net resale equity (Resale Value − Remaining Loan Balance) over the identical month-N horizon.

  • Models monthly-stream sales tax on lease payments rather than upfront full-price lease taxation (TX/IL/NY variations).
  • Assumes identical routine maintenance/insurance and no excess mileage penalties ($0.25–$0.30/mi) or wear-and-tear charges.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you convert a car lease Money Factor into an approximate APR?

Multiply the dealer’s Money Factor by 2,400. For example, a 0.0025 Money Factor equals 0.0025 × 2,400 = 6.00% equivalent annual financing rate applied to both the capitalized cost and residual value.

How does this calculator keep the 36-month Lease vs. Buy comparison apples-to-apples?

Instead of penalizing the buyer for building ownership equity, the engine amortizes the auto loan over its full term, takes the exact remaining payoff balance at month 36, and credits Net Resale Equity (Market Value minus Loan Payoff) against total cash spent.

What hidden car lease costs can widen the gap beyond this model?

Over-mileage penalties ($0.15–$0.30/mile above 10k–12k miles/yr), excess wear-and-tear charges at turn-in, and disposition fees increase net leasing costs if you drive heavily or keep vehicles beyond 3 years.
