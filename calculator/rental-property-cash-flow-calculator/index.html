How It Works & Mathematical Principles

The Rental Property Cash Flow Calculator performs full residential underwriting from Gross Scheduled Rent down to Effective Gross Income (after vacancy), Operating Expenses, Net Operating Income (NOI), Cap Rate, Annual Debt Service, DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio), and Cash-on-Cash Return.

Listing websites routinely advertise misleading gross rental yields that exclude property taxes, landlord insurance, routine maintenance, professional management fees, and long-term CapEx reserves (roofs, HVAC, water heaters). Structuring real estate analysis cleanly into EGI → OpEx → NOI → Debt Service reveals whether a leveraged purchase cash-flows positively or requires monthly out-of-pocket subsidies.

Governing Equation
EGI = GrossRent(1 − Vac%) | NOI = EGI − OpEx | Cap Rate = NOI ÷ Price | Cash Flow = NOI − Debt | DSCR = NOI ÷ Debt

Step-by-Step Worked Calculation Example

Scenario: Evaluating a $360,000 rental (20% down = $72,000, $10,800 closing/rehab = $82,800 cash in; 6.5% 30-yr loan = $288,000) renting for $2,850/mo with 5% vacancy, $4,200 tax, $1,600 ins, $2,400 maint, 8% mgmt, and $1,800 CapEx.

  • Effective Gross Income = ($2,850 × 12) × (1 − 0.05) = $34,200 × 0.95 = $32,490.00/yr.
  • Total OpEx = $4,200 (tax) + $1,600 (ins) + $2,400 (maint) + $2,599.20 (8% mgmt) + $1,800 (CapEx) = $12,599.20/yr.
  • NOI = $32,490.00 − $12,599.20 = $19,890.80 (Cap Rate = $19,890.80 ÷ $360,000 = 5.53%).
  • Mortgage P&I on $288,000 @ 6.5% (30 yrs) = $1,820.36/mo ($21,844.27/yr) → Pre-Tax Cash Flow = -$1,953.47/yr (-$162.79/mo; Cash-on-Cash = -2.36%; DSCR = 0.91x).

Result: -$162.79 / month Pre-Tax Cash Flow (5.53% Cap Rate · 0.91x DSCR)

Notice negative leverage: because the 6.5% borrowing rate exceeds the unlevered 5.53% Cap Rate (and DSCR is below 1.0x), the property drains $162.79 monthly unless price is negotiated down, rent rises, or down payment increases.

Regional Standards & Units

Tax treatment of rental losses and Stamp Duty varies cleanly across target property markets:

  • United States: 27.5-yr straight-line depreciation per IRS Pub 527; 1031 exchanges allow capital gain deferral on like-kind rollovers. (USD ($)) [2026]
  • United Kingdom: Section 24 restricts mortgage interest relief to a 20% basic-rate tax credit; additional 5% SDLT surcharge on buy-to-let. (GBP (£)) [2026–27]
  • Canada: Mortgage interest deductible; CCA building depreciation optional (stops at zero net income) and recaptured on sale. (CAD (C$)) [2026]
  • Australia: Negative gearing allows net rental cash + Div 40/43 depreciation losses to offset ordinary PAYG taxable salary. (AUD (A$)) [FY 2026–27]

Omitting CapEx Reserves and Vacancy Allowance ("100% Occupancy Fallacy")

Evaluating Rent − Mortgage P&I alone overlooks roofs ($12k+), HVAC ($7k+), turnover cleaning, and 1 month empty every two years—turning "paper profits" into negative real returns.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the calculator separate CapEx Reserve from annual Repairs & Maintenance?

Routine maintenance fixes minor leaks and painting each year, whereas Capital Expenditures (CapEx) accrue reserves for heavy multi-decade replacements like roofs, HVAC compressors, and driveways.

What does a DSCR below 1.0x mean?

A Debt Service Coverage Ratio under 1.0x (such as 0.91x in our default leveraged example) means property Net Operating Income does not cover annual mortgage P&I payments—requiring monthly cash subsidies and failing typical DSCR investment loan thresholds (1.20x–1.25x).

How does Cap Rate differ from Cash-on-Cash Return?

Cap Rate (NOI ÷ Price) evaluates unlevered property operating yield independent of mortgage debt, while Cash-on-Cash Return (Pre-Tax Cash Flow ÷ Total Cash Invested) measures actual leveraged annual cash yield on your down payment + closing/rehab cash.
