How It Works & Mathematical Principles

The Wedding Budget Calculator breaks a total target wedding budget down by per-guest unit cost and standard event spending categories—Venue & Catering, Photography & Video, Attire & Beauty, Music/Florals/Decor, Stationery/Misc, and a 10% Contingency Buffer—while computing the exact monthly savings needed by your wedding date.

Because catering, bar packages, rentals, stationery, and favors scale linearly with headcount, measuring Cost Per Guest is the single fastest lever for controlling total spend. Equally important is carving out an explicit 10% contingency reserve from Day 1 so late vendor overtime, service charges, or tax additions never spill onto post-wedding credit cards.

Step-by-Step Worked Calculation Example

Scenario: Planning a $32,000 wedding for 120 guests in 14 months, with $12,000 already saved and $6,000 in family contributions across standard 45% / 12% / 8% / 15% / 10% / 10% allocations.

  • Cost Per Guest = $32,000.00 ÷ 120 = $266.67 per attendee.
  • Total Committed Funding = $12,000 + $6,000 = $18,000.00; Remaining Cash Gap = $32,000 − $18,000 = $14,000.00.
  • Monthly Savings Required = $14,000.00 ÷ 14 months = $1,000.00/month.
  • Category Caps: Venue & Catering $14,400 (45%), Music & Decor $4,800 (15%), Photo & Video $3,840 (12%), Misc $3,200 (10%), Contingency $3,200 (10%), Attire $2,560 (8%).

Result: $1,000.00 / month Required Savings ($266.67 Cost Per Guest)

Trimming 20 guests at $266.67/guest could save ~$3,500–$5,300 directly in per-head catering and bar costs, immediately lowering required monthly savings by ~$250–$380.

Regional Standards & Units

Venue tax/service-charge quotation norms vary slightly across markets:

  • United States & Canada: Catering quotes typically exclude 18%–22% service fees and sales/HST tax—factor total invoice price into the 45% cap. (USD / CAD) [2026]
  • United Kingdom & AU/NZ: Consumer prices usually include 20% VAT or 10%–15% GST upfront—verify whether corkage or weekend surcharges apply. (GBP / AUD / NZD) [2026]

Forgetting Plus-Service Charges and Final Vendor Payment Milestones

Assuming a $100/plate quote costs $12,000 for 120 guests ignores ~20% service fees and 7%–13% tax that push the real bill past $15,200.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why must all six wedding category percentages add up to 100%?

Requiring weights to sum cleanly to 100%—including a dedicated Contingency Buffer—ensures expanding one tier like Venue & Catering explicitly trades off against another without hidden budget creep.

Do catering quotes already include tax and service charges?

In the US and Canada, catering contracts typically exclude 18%–22% mandatory service charges plus local sales/HST tax. Allocate your 45% Venue & Catering share based on final all-in invoice estimates.

How does guest count drive wedding affordability?

Catering, open bar, rentals, stationery, and favors scale per head—so trimming 20 attendees at $266.67/guest reduces total spend by over $5,300 at the default $32,000 / 120-guest benchmark.
