How It Works & Mathematical Principles

The NPV, IRR & Payback Period Calculator evaluates 5-year capital budgeting projects—computing Net Present Value (NPV), Internal Rate of Return (IRR via numerical root-finding), Profitability Index (PI), Simple Payback, and Discounted Payback against your WACC hurdle rate.

Net Present Value (NPV) measures absolute dollar wealth created above your cost of capital (WACC), whereas Internal Rate of Return (IRR) finds the exact discount rate where NPV equals zero. Using both alongside Profitability Index and Discounted Payback prevents misranking mutually exclusive projects of different scales.

Governing Equation
NPV = −I₀ + Σ CF_t ÷ (1 + r)^t | NPV(IRR) = 0 | PI = PV(Inflows) ÷ I₀

Step-by-Step Worked Calculation Example

Scenario: Evaluating a $100,000 capital investment at a 10.0% WACC hurdle generating Years 1–5 cash flows of $28,000, $34,000, $38,000, $40,000, and $42,000.

  • PV of Inflows @ 10%: $25,454.55 + $28,099.17 + $28,549.96 + $27,320.54 + $26,078.70 = $135,502.92.
  • Net Present Value (NPV) = $135,502.92 − $100,000.00 = +$35,502.92 (Profitability Index = 1.355x → Accept).
  • Solving NPV(r) = 0 gives IRR = 22.14% (+12.14% spread above the 10% hurdle).
  • Simple Payback = 3.00 years ($28k + $34k + $38k = $100k); Discounted Payback = 3.66 years.

Result: +$35,502.92 NPV | 22.14% IRR | 1.355x PI (Accept Project)

The investment generates $35,502.92 of economic value above a 10% cost of capital, returning invested capital in 3.66 discounted years.

Discounts discrete end-of-year cash flows CF₁..CF₅ at WACC, solves IRR where NPV(r) = 0 across [-95%, +500%], and interpolates exact simple and discounted payback years.

Frequently Asked Questions

When NPV and IRR disagree on mutually exclusive capital projects, which metric wins?

Always follow Net Present Value (NPV). IRR assumes intermediate cash inflows reinvest at the project’s own IRR—which can exaggerate small, fast-payback projects—whereas NPV measures exact absolute dollar wealth created above your WACC hurdle rate.

Why does Discounted Payback Period take longer than Simple Payback Period?

Simple Payback sums nominal future cash flows without discounting for time value of money. Discounted Payback shrinks each future year’s cash flow by (1 + WACC)^t before checking when initial capital I₀ is recovered.

How should I interpret Profitability Index (PI)?

Profitability Index equals (NPV + Initial Investment) ÷ Initial Investment. Projects with PI > 1.0x create positive value above the hurdle rate, making PI ideal for ranking competing investments under tight capital budgets.
