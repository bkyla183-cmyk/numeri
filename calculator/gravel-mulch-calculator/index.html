How It Works & Mathematical Principles

The Mulch, Soil & Gravel Volume Calculator computes exact bulk Cubic Yards (yd³), Metric Cubic Meters (m³), Cubic Feet, US Short Tons, Metric Tonnes, and bagged equivalents (e.g., 2 ft³ bags) across hardwood mulch, screened topsoil, crushed gravel, and masonry sand.

Landscape supply yards sell bulk materials by the Cubic Yard (27 cubic feet) or by weight (US Tons), whereas garden centers sell bags by the Cubic Foot (commonly 1.5, 2.0, or 3.0 ft³). A single cubic yard equals 13.5 standard 2-cubic-foot bags—making bulk delivery dramatically cheaper above 2–3 yards, provided you verify payload weight limits for pickup trucks or dump trailers.

Step-by-Step Worked Calculation Example

Scenario: Top-dressing a 600 sq ft garden bed with 3 inches of shredded hardwood bark mulch (~800 lb/yd³), comparing bulk delivery against 2 ft³ bags.

  • Cubic Feet = 600 × (3 ÷ 12) = 150.0 ft³.
  • Bulk Volume = 150.0 ÷ 27 = 5.56 cubic yards (4.25 m³).
  • Weight & Bags: 5.56 × 800 = 4,444 lb (2.22 US Tons / 2.02 metric tonnes); Bagged Alternative = ceil(150 ÷ 2.0) = 75 bags.

Result: 5.56 Cubic Yards Bulk Volume (2.22 US Tons · 4.25 m³ · 75 Bags)

Buying 5.56 yd³ in bulk saves hauling 75 plastic bags and usually cuts material cost by 40%–60%, though a 2.22-ton load exceeds a half-ton pickup’s single-trip payload.

Regional Standards & Units

Provides bulk cubic yards / US short tons alongside metric cubic meters (m³) and tonnes (t):

  • United States & Canada: Sold in cubic yards (yd³), 2–3 ft³ retail bags, and US Short Tons (2,000 lb). (yd³ & Short Tons) [2026 MSC]
  • UK / Australia / New Zealand: Landscape quarries sell in cubic meters (m³), 850 kg dumpy bags, and metric tonnes (1,000 kg). (m³ & Tonnes) [2026]

Confusing Square Feet With Cubic Yards or Overloading Pickup Axles With Wet Gravel

Always check weight before picking up bulk rock or wet soil yourself: 2 yards of crushed limestone weighs ~5,600 lb and requires a tandem dump trailer or yard delivery.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many 2-cubic-foot bags equal one bulk cubic yard?

One cubic yard equals exactly 27 cubic feet—so 1 yd³ = 13.5 standard 2-cubic-foot bags (or 9 large 3-cubic-foot bags). Buying bulk delivery is usually much cheaper above 2–3 cubic yards.

How much does a cubic yard of crushed gravel weigh compared to mulch?

Hardwood bark mulch averages ~800 lb/yd³, screened topsoil ~2,200 lb/yd³, and crushed gravel ~2,800 lb/yd³ (1.4 US Tons per yard). Always check pickup truck axle/payload ratings before loading stone in bulk.

How deep should I spread landscape mulch vs. driveway gravel?

Spread garden mulch 2–3 inches deep for moisture retention without suffocating plant roots; base layers of crushed stone for patios or driveways typically run 3–6 inches compacted.
