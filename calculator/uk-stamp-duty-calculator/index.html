How It Works & Mathematical Principles

The UK Stamp Duty (SDLT) Calculator computes exact Stamp Duty Land Tax owed on residential property purchases in England and Northern Ireland using HMRC’s post-April 2025 statutory bands active throughout 2026–27, including First-Time Buyer Relief, the +5% Additional Property surcharge, and the +2% Non-UK Resident surcharge.

Following the expiry of temporary nil-rate reliefs on 1 April 2025, standard England and Northern Ireland residential SDLT begins at 2% above £125,000, 5% above £250,000, 10% above £925,000, and 12% above £1.5M. Meanwhile, First-Time Buyer relief applies 0% up to £300,000 and 5% from £300,001 to £500,000—but forfeits entirely if the price exceeds £500,000.

Step-by-Step Worked Calculation Example

Scenario: Purchasing a £425,000 main residence in England during 2026–27 as a Standard Home Mover (UK resident).

  • Slice 1 (£0 to £125,000 @ 0%) = £0.00.
  • Slice 2 (£125,001 to £250,000 = £125,000 @ 2%) = £2,500.00.
  • Slice 3 (£250,001 to £425,000 = £175,000 @ 5%) = £8,750.00.
  • Total SDLT = £0 + £2,500 + £8,750 = £11,250.00 (2.65% effective rate; £436,250 total cash incl. price). Had the buyer been an eligible First-Time Buyer, SDLT would drop to £6,250.00.

Result: £11,250.00 England & NI SDLT Payable (2.65% Effective Rate)

SDLT is marginal (slice-based) rather than a cliff—except when crossing £500,000 as a First-Time Buyer, which disqualifies the entire FTB relief.

Regional Standards & Units

Applies strictly to England & Northern Ireland SDLT; note separate devolved UK nations:

  • England & Northern Ireland: HMRC Stamp Duty Land Tax (SDLT) with post-April 2025 £125k nil-rate threshold and £500k FTB ceiling. (GBP (£)) [2026–27]
  • Scotland (LBTT) & Wales (LTT): Scotland administers Land & Buildings Transaction Tax (LBTT) via Revenue Scotland; Wales administers Land Transaction Tax (LTT) via WRA with distinct bands. (GBP (£)) [2026–27]

Paying £505,000 as a First-Time Buyer and Losing the Entire Relief

At £500,000 a First-Time Buyer pays £10,000 SDLT, whereas at £500,001 FTB relief vanishes completely and SDLT jumps immediately to £15,000.05—a £5,000 cliff on £1 of price.

Frequently Asked Questions

What UK Stamp Duty (SDLT) thresholds apply in England and Northern Ireland for 2026–27?

Following the April 2025 threshold reversion active throughout 2026–27, standard buyers pay 0% up to £125,000, 2% on £125,001–£250,000, 5% on £250,001–£925,000, 10% on £925,001–£1.5M, and 12% above £1.5M.

What happens if a First-Time Buyer purchases a home for £500,001 instead of £500,000?

UK First-Time Buyer Relief (0% up to £300,000 and 5% from £300,001 to £500,000) is subject to a strict £500,000 cliff edge. At £500,000, SDLT is £10,000; at £500,001, relief is forfeited entirely and standard bands apply (£15,000+).

How do the 5% Additional Property and 2% Non-UK Resident surcharges stack?

Both surcharges are additive across every price slice (for purchases £40,000+): an overseas landlord buying a second home or Buy-to-Let property pays standard SDLT rates plus 7% (5% additional dwelling + 2% non-resident) on the entire price.
