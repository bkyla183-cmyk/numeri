How It Works & Mathematical Principles

The Cost of Living Comparison Calculator calculates the equivalent target-city salary needed to maintain your current purchasing power, supporting both a detailed five-category household weighted basket (Housing, Groceries, Transport, Utilities/Healthcare, Discretionary) and a single headline city index ratio.

Comparing headline cost-of-living index scores between cities often misleads job seekers because housing costs vary far more dramatically between metro areas than groceries or utilities. A household spending 35% of income on rent in a tier-2 city faces a very different relocation budget shift than a household spending 45% in London, San Francisco, Toronto, or Sydney. Weighting each category index directly by your real budget shares delivers an individualized equivalent salary.

Governing Equation
Composite Index = Σ(w_j × Index_j) ÷ Σw_j | Equivalent Salary = Salary × (Composite ÷ 100)

Step-by-Step Worked Calculation Example

Scenario: Evaluating a relocation offer on an $85,000 salary when target Housing index is 145 (+45%), Groceries 120, Transport 115, Utilities 110, and Other 115 under weights 35%/15%/15%/10%/25%.

  • Weighted sum = (35 × 145) + (15 × 120) + (15 × 115) + (10 × 110) + (25 × 115) = 5,075 + 1,800 + 1,725 + 1,100 + 2,875 = 12,575.
  • Composite Cost Index = 12,575 ÷ 100 = 125.75 (+25.75% higher personal basket cost).
  • Equivalent Target Salary = $85,000 × (125.75 ÷ 100) = $106,887.50 (+$21,887.50 raise required; $8,907.29/mo gross equivalent).

Result: $106,887.50 Weighted Equivalent Salary (+25.75% · Composite Index 125.75)

Accepting any relocation offer below $106,887.50 reduces real purchasing power—and even more so if moving into a higher state/provincial income tax jurisdiction.

Regional Standards & Units

Budget weights shift visibly across car-centric North American/Australian suburbs vs. transit-heavy metro hubs:

  • United States: Check both housing price index and state income tax shift alongside car ownership vs. urban transit shares. (USD ($)) [2026]
  • United Kingdom: London vs. Manchester/Edinburgh differentials are dominated by housing rent and Council Tax bands. (GBP (£)) [2026–27]
  • Canada: Toronto/Vancouver vs. Calgary/Montreal differences center on shelter, provincial PST/HST, and auto insurance. (CAD (C$)) [2026]
  • Australia: Sydney vs. Brisbane/Adelaide spreads reflect inner-metro rent and childcare gaps. (AUD (A$)) [2026]

Ignoring Commute Mode Shifts or Progressive Tax Bracket Jumps

A +25.75% nominal salary raise can leave net disposable cash lower if your marginal tax bracket jumps simultaneously or if a city requires adding a car loan, fuel, and insurance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is a weighted category basket more accurate than a headline city cost index?

Housing varies far more between cities than groceries or utilities. If housing consumes 40%–45% of your personal budget, weighting Housing directly avoids understating the raise needed when moving to high-rent hubs.

Does this account for state, provincial, or national income tax changes?

The primary calculation outputs gross salary equivalent; always pair it with our Income Tax or Take-Home Pay Calculator if moving between low-tax and high-tax jurisdictions.

What if I only have one overall index number for each city?

Switch the Comparison Methodology dropdown to "Single Headline City Index Ratio" to convert your salary directly using any official regional parity or city index score.
