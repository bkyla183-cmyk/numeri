How It Works & Mathematical Principles

The RMD Calculator determines your official IRS Required Minimum Distribution using the full IRS Publication 590-B Table III (Uniform Lifetime Table, ages 72–120+) and SECURE 2.0 Act starting age rules (Age 73 for 1951–1959 births; Age 75 for 1960+), while properly exempting Roth IRAs and Designated Roth 401(k)/403(b) accounts during the owner’s lifetime.

Under SECURE 2.0 changes, failing to withdraw your exact minimum IRS distribution by December 31 triggers a steep 25% excise tax on the shortfall (reduced to 10% if corrected promptly during the two-year correction window). Equally important: starting in 2024+, Designated Roth 401(k) and 403(b) accounts joined Roth IRAs in having zero lifetime RMDs, freeing retirees from unnecessary forced withdrawals.

Step-by-Step Worked Calculation Example

Scenario: A Traditional IRA owner born in 1952 (age 74 in 2026) with a December 31, 2025 balance of $650,000 computing their 2026 RMD.

  • Birth year 1952 maps to SECURE 2.0 RMD starting age 73 (first RMD tax year was 2025); Owner Age in 2026 = 2026 − 1952 = 74.
  • IRS Publication 590-B Table III Uniform Lifetime distribution period for Age 74 = 25.5.
  • 2026 RMD = $650,000.00 ÷ 25.5 = $25,490.20 (3.9216% of prior Dec 31 balance; $2,124.18/mo equivalent).
  • Because 2026 is the owner’s second RMD year, the mandatory withdrawal deadline is December 31, 2026 (April 1 delay applies only to the very first RMD year 2025).

Result: $25,490.20 Required Minimum Distribution for 2026 (Factor 25.5)

Distributing at least $25,490.20 before December 31, 2026 satisfies IRS rules and avoids the 25% missed-RMD excise penalty.

Regional Standards & Units

US IRS RMD law compared with equivalent mandatory withdrawal ages in Canada, UK, and Australia:

  • United States: IRS Pub 590-B Table III RMDs starting Age 73 (1951–1959) or 75 (1960+); $0 lifetime RMD on Roth accounts. (USD ($)) [2026 Tax Year]
  • Canada (Comparison): Canadian RRSPs must convert to a RRIF by Dec 31 of age 71 with mandatory prescribed percentage minimums starting at 72. (CAD (C$)) [2026]
  • Australia (Comparison): Account-Based Pensions carry ATO age-bracket minimum drawdowns (4% under 65, 5% age 65–74, 6% age 75–79). (AUD (A$)) [FY 2026–27]

Delaying Your First RMD to April 1 Without Preparing for Double Taxation

Postponing your very first RMD until April 1 of the following year forces taking TWO RMDs in the same calendar year—potentially pushing income into a higher tax bracket and raising Medicare IRMAA surcharges.

Frequently Asked Questions

At what age do RMDs begin under SECURE 2.0 in 2026?

Owners born in 1950 or earlier began at 72 (or 70½ prior); owners born 1951–1959 begin at Age 73; owners born 1960 or later begin at Age 75.

Do Designated Roth 401(k) or Roth 403(b) accounts still have RMDs?

No. Under SECURE 2.0 rules effective from 2024 onward, BOTH Roth IRAs and Designated Roth 401(k)/403(b) plans have $0 Required Minimum Distributions during the original owner’s lifetime.

What is the penalty for missing an RMD deadline?

Missing your December 31 deadline triggers a 25% IRS excise tax on the un-withdrawn shortfall—reduced to 10% if corrected promptly within the IRS correction window.
