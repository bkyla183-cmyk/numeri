How It Works & Mathematical Principles

The Net Worth Calculator establishes your single clearest personal balance sheet snapshot by subtracting total liabilities from total assets, while simultaneously isolating Liquid Net Worth and Net Home Equity so illiquid property wealth does not mask consumer debt pressure.

Total Net Worth measures long-term wealth accumulation across cash, brokerage and retirement accounts, real estate, and personal property net of mortgages, auto loans, student debt, and credit cards. However, two households with identical $280,000 net worth can face completely different financial flexibility if one holds $250,000 in locked home equity and $40,000 in credit card debt versus holding $163,000 in liquid cash and investments.

Governing Equation
Net Worth = Σ Assets − Σ Liabilities | Liquid Net Worth = (Cash + Investments) − Non-Mortgage Debt

Step-by-Step Worked Calculation Example

Scenario: Evaluating a homeowner holding $18,000 cash, $145,000 investments/retirement, $420,000 home value, and $17,000 other assets against a $285,000 mortgage, $18,000 auto loan, $12,000 student loan, and $5,000 cards.

  • Total Assets = $18,000 + $145,000 + $420,000 + $17,000 = $600,000.00.
  • Total Liabilities = $285,000 + $18,000 + $12,000 + $5,000 = $320,000.00.
  • Total Net Worth = $600,000.00 − $320,000.00 = $280,000.00 (Debt-to-Asset Ratio = 53.33%).
  • Liquid Net Worth = ($18,000 + $145,000) − ($18,000 + $12,000 + $5,000) = $163,000 − $35,000 = $128,000.00; Net Home Equity = $135,000.00.

Result: $280,000.00 Total Net Worth ($128,000.00 Liquid Net Worth)

While home equity contributes $135,000 (48.2% of total net worth), strong liquid holdings ($163,000) exceed non-mortgage liabilities by nearly 4.7×, keeping balance-sheet risk well controlled.

Regional Standards & Units

Balance-sheet items follow identical accounting subtraction across markets with localized account wrappers:

  • United States: Include 401(k), IRA, HSA, brokerage, and equity; exclude primary residence when testing SEC Accredited status. (USD ($)) [2026]
  • United Kingdom: Include ISAs, SIPP/workplace DC pensions, and property market value minus outstanding mortgage. (GBP (£)) [2026–27]
  • Canada: Include RRSP, TFSA, FHSA, RESP, and home equity against HELOC, mortgage, and consumer balances. (CAD (C$)) [2026]
  • Australia: Include Superannuation balance, offset cash, and property minus HECS-HELP and mortgage debt. (AUD (A$)) [2026]

Overstating Depreciating Vehicles or Ignoring Selling Costs

Recording purchase price instead of realistic trade-in value for cars and electronics—or forgetting 5%–7% real estate transaction costs—systematically inflates true realizable net worth.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Total Net Worth and Liquid Net Worth?

Total Net Worth counts all assets (including home equity and illiquid possessions) minus all debts, whereas Liquid Net Worth isolates Cash + Investments minus non-mortgage debt to reveal true cash flexibility.

Should retirement accounts like 401(k)s, RRSPs, or Super be listed pre-tax?

Yes, consumer balance-sheet standards (including Federal Reserve SCF) record retirement and brokerage balances at current statement market value while keeping in mind that pre-tax wrappers incur ordinary tax at withdrawal.

Why does SEC Accredited Investor status exclude primary home equity?

Under US SEC rules, the $1M net worth test excludes primary residence equity (and treats excess mortgage debt above fair market value as a liability) so retirees are not qualified solely on illiquid housing appreciation.
