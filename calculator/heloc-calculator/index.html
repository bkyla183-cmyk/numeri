How It Works & Mathematical Principles

The HELOC Payment & Equity Calculator models a Home Equity Line of Credit across both its interest-only Draw Period and full P&I Repayment Period while checking Combined Loan-to-Value (CLTV) borrowing limits against your home value.

Many borrowers budget only for the low interest-only minimum payment during a 10-year HELOC draw period and get blindsided by the repayment payment shock when principal amortization begins in Year 11. This calculator quantifies both phases, calculates exact CLTV eligibility (typically capped at 80%–85%), and reports total borrowing headroom.

Governing Equation
Draw_MO = Draw × (APR ÷ 12) | Repay_P&I = Draw × [r(1+r)^n ÷ ((1+r)^n − 1)] | CLTV = (Mtg + Draw) ÷ HomeValue

Step-by-Step Worked Calculation Example

Scenario: Home valued at $550,000 with a $290,000 first mortgage; drawing $85,000 on an 8.25% APR HELOC (10-yr interest-only draw, 20-yr repayment, 85% max CLTV).

  • CLTV = ($290,000 + $85,000) ÷ $550,000 = 68.18% (well within 85% max CLTV; max borrowing power = $177,500.00).
  • Phase 1 Draw Period (Years 1–10): $85,000 × (8.25% ÷ 12) = $584.38/mo interest-only ($70,125.00 interest over 120 months).
  • Phase 2 Repayment Period (Years 11–30 / 240 mos): P&I jumps to $724.26/mo (+$139.88/mo reset shock), adding $88,821.39 interest.

Result: $724.26/mo Repayment P&I (up from $584.38/mo Draw) | 68.18% CLTV

While 68.18% CLTV easily qualifies under an 85% equity cap, carrying the $85,000 draw for all 10 interest-only years plus 20 repayment years generates $158,946.39 in cumulative interest.

Regional Standards & Units

Models US HELOCs, Canadian Home Equity Lines (Smith Manoeuvre / Re-advanceable Mortgages), and Australian Line-of-Credit facilities:

  • United States: Standard 10-yr interest-only draw + 20-yr P&I repayment at Prime + Margin variable APR. (USD ($)) [2026]
  • Canada: OSFI B-20 caps HELOC revolving portion at 65% LTV (80% total LTV combined with amortizing mortgage). (CAD (C$)) [2026]

Treating Variable HELOC Rates as Fixed and Ignoring Draw-End Payment Shock

Every 1% rise in the Prime rate immediately increases an $85,000 interest-only draw payment by ~$70.83/mo, and principal amortization jumps payments sharply when the draw ends.

Frequently Asked Questions

What causes the HELOC payment reset shock when the draw period ends?

During a typical 10-year draw period, many Home Equity Lines of Credit only require interest-only payments (Balance × APR ÷ 12). When the 20-year repayment period begins, the line freezes and amortizes full principal plus interest, jumping monthly payments significantly.

How do lenders calculate Combined Loan-to-Value (CLTV) for HELOC approval?

Lenders divide the sum of your existing first mortgage balance plus your requested HELOC credit limit by your appraised home value: CLTV = (Mortgage + HELOC) ÷ Home Value. Most US and Canadian lenders cap CLTV between 80% and 85%.

When is HELOC interest tax-deductible in the United States?

Under IRC §163(h)(3) rules, HELOC interest is only deductible on Schedule A when funds are used to buy, build, or substantially improve the qualified residence securing the loan, subject to the overall mortgage debt limit—not when spent on debt consolidation or personal expenses.
