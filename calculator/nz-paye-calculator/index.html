How It Works & Mathematical Principles

The New Zealand PAYE & KiwiSaver Calculator computes 2026–27 net take-home pay using Inland Revenue (IRD) income tax brackets, the 1.75% ACC Earners’ Levy (capped at NZ$156,641 liable earnings), Independent Earner Tax Credit (IETC), KiwiSaver deductions, and the 12% NZ Student Loan threshold (NZ$24,128).

New Zealand deducts progressive PAYE income tax across five bands (10.5% to 39%), a flat 1.75% ACC Earners’ Levy up to NZ$156,641 (NZ$2,741.22 max in 2026–27), employee KiwiSaver savings (minimum 3%), and 12% Student Loan repayments on every dollar above NZ$24,128.

Governing Equation
Net = Gross − PAYE(10.5%–39%) + IETC − min(Gross, 156,641)×1.75% − KiwiSaver − 12%×max(0, Gross−24,128)

Step-by-Step Worked Calculation Example

Scenario: New Zealand employee earning NZ$85,000/yr in 2026–27 with 3% KiwiSaver and an active NZ Student Loan (SL tax code).

  • IRD 2026–27 PAYE: 10.5% ($15.6k = $1,638) + 17.5% ($37.9k = $6,632.50) + 30% ($24.6k = $7,380) + 33% ($6.9k = $2,277) = NZ$17,927.50.
  • 2026–27 ACC Earners’ Levy: NZ$85,000 × 1.75% = NZ$1,487.50.
  • KiwiSaver (3%) = NZ$2,550.00/yr; Student Loan: (NZ$85,000 − NZ$24,128) × 12% = NZ$7,304.64/yr.
  • Annual Net Pay = NZ$55,730.36/yr (NZ$2,143.48/fortnight | NZ$4,644.20/mo).

Result: NZ$55,730.36/yr Net Take-Home Pay (NZ$2,143.48/fortnight)

On NZ$85,000, IRD PAYE and ACC take 22.84% while KiwiSaver (3%) and the 12% student loan obligation account for another NZ$9,854.64 in payroll deductions.

Ignoring Employer ESCT Tax When Checking Total KiwiSaver Growth

Your 3% employee KiwiSaver comes out of after-tax net pay, whereas your employer’s matching 3% contribution incurs Employer Superannuation Contribution Tax (ESCT) before entering your account.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the 2026–27 New Zealand IRD PAYE income tax brackets and ACC levy?

IRD PAYE tiers are 10.5% (≤NZ$15,600), 17.5% (to NZ$53,500), 30% (to NZ$78,100), 33% (to NZ$180,000), and 39% (>NZ$180,000). The 2026–27 ACC Earners’ Levy is 1.75% capped at NZ$156,641 maximum liable earnings (NZ$2,741.22 max).

How do KiwiSaver and the Independent Earner Tax Credit (IETC) work in this model?

Employees choosing 3%, 4%, 6%, 8%, or 10% KiwiSaver see their deduction taken from after-tax pay (alongside a mandatory 3% employer contribution before ESCT). Earners between NZ$24,000 and NZ$70,000 also receive up to NZ$520/yr in IETC relief.

When does New Zealand Student Loan PAYE withholding apply?

Borrowers on an SL tax code pay 12% on gross earnings exceeding the NZ$24,128 annual repayment threshold (NZ$464/week or NZ$928/fortnight), deducted automatically alongside PAYE and ACC.
