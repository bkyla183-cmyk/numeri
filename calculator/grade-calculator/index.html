How It Works & Mathematical Principles

A Grade Percentage to Letter Calculator converts numerical test or final course percentages into standard US letter grades (A+ through F), 4.0 grade-point equivalents, and academic standing tiers.

Translating raw exam scores (for example 88.5%) onto standardized letter-grade scales clarifies how close an assignment sits to the next GPA tier before final transcripts lock.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are percentage scores mapped to letter grades and 4.0 GPA points?

Standard US academic grading typically maps 93%–100% to A (4.0), 90%–92.9% to A− (3.7), 87%–89.9% to B+ (3.3), 83%–86.9% to B (3.0), down to below 60% as F (0.0).

Do all schools use the exact same plus/minus grading cutoffs?

No. Some universities omit plus/minus steps (using flat A=4.0, B=3.0) or treat A− as 3.67 instead of 3.7—always verify against your institution’s registrar syllabus.

How do I calculate my raw test percentage?

Divide Points Earned by Total Possible Points and multiply by 100.
