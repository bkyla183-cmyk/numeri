How It Works & Mathematical Principles

The Tax-Loss Harvesting Savings Calculator computes immediate current-year tax savings from selling unrealized loss positions under IRS IRC §1211/§1212 netting rules—offsetting Short-Term Gains first, then Long-Term Gains, then up to $3,000 ($1,500 MFS) of ordinary income, and projecting indefinite loss carryforwards.

Tax-loss harvesting turns temporary portfolio pullbacks into immediate tax alpha by offsetting high-taxed short-term gains (taxed up to 37% ordinary rates) and up to $3,000 of W-2 wages each year while carrying remaining losses forward indefinitely. To preserve the deduction, investors must avoid the 61-day Wash-Sale window (±30 days around the loss sale).

Step-by-Step Worked Calculation Example

Scenario: Harvesting an $18,000 unrealized capital loss in a year with $6,000 short-term gains (24% rate) and $5,000 long-term gains (15% rate) as a Single/MFJ filer ($3,000 ordinary cap).

  • Step 1 — Offset Short-Term Gains: $6,000 × 24% = $1,440.00 saved ($12,000 loss remaining).
  • Step 2 — Offset Long-Term Gains: $5,000 × 15% = $750.00 saved ($7,000 loss remaining).
  • Step 3 — Ordinary Income Offset (IRC §1211(b) cap): $3,000 × 24% = $720.00 saved ($4,000 carryforward left).
  • Total Immediate Tax Savings = $1,440 + $750 + $720 = $2,910.00 + $4,000 indefinite loss carryforward (~$600 future shield at 15%).

Result: $2,910.00 Current-Year Federal Tax Savings + $4,000.00 Carryforward

Harvesting $18,000 trims this year’s IRS bill by $2,910 immediately while banking $4,000 of tax losses for next year—just avoid buying a substantially identical security within ±30 days.

Rebuying an Identical ETF or Stock Inside Your IRA Within 30 Days

Under IRS Revenue Ruling 2008-5, selling a stock at a loss in a taxable brokerage account and buying the same stock inside a Traditional or Roth IRA within 30 days triggers a permanent Wash Sale disallowance.

Frequently Asked Questions

In what mandatory IRS order do harvested capital losses offset gains and income?

Under IRC §1211 and §1212, net capital losses first offset same-bucket gains and remaining capital gains dollar-for-dollar (offsetting high-taxed short-term gains before preferential long-term gains when net losses remain), then deduct up to $3,000/yr ($1,500 Married Filing Separately) against ordinary income, carrying excess indefinitely forward.

What is the 30-day IRS Wash-Sale Rule (61-day window) and how do investors avoid it?

Under IRC §1091, you cannot deduct a harvested loss if you or a spouse/IRA buy the same or "substantially identical" security within 30 calendar days before OR 30 days after the loss sale (61 days total). Many investors temporarily rotate into a non-identical broad index or peer sector ETF during those 31 days.

Does tax-loss harvesting permanently erase taxes or defer them?

Harvesting lowers your current-year tax bill immediately while resetting the cost basis of the replacement asset lower. Wealth benefits come from earning compounded returns on reinvested tax savings, shifting short-term gain offsets into future long-term rates, or receiving a stepped-up basis at estate transfer.
